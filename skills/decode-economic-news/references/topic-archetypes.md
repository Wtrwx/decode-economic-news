# Topic Archetypes

Select one primary archetype and add others only when necessary.

## Company strategy

Trace revenue model, resource allocation, control rights, financing needs, founder incentives, competitive response and execution constraints. Prefer filings and company statements over media summaries.

## Industrial competition

Trace cost curves, supply-chain density, capacity, learning speed, standards, market access and policy protection. Distinguish one company's execution from industry-wide structural advantage.

## Policy game

Trace policy objective, legal instrument, affected actors, compliance path, retaliation options and implementation timeline. Separate official text from political interpretation.

## Macro transmission

Trace shock, prices, income, demand, production, trade, fiscal/monetary response and distributional effects. Check units, seasonality, base effects and revisions.

## Capital market

Trace expectation, positioning, liquidity, valuation, catalyst and realized fundamentals. Price movement is evidence of repricing, not proof of the underlying story.

## Mixed event

Choose one dominant causal chain. Use secondary archetypes only for downstream effects so the script does not become a list of unrelated facts.
