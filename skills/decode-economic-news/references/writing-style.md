# Writing Style

Apply this only after evidence validation.

## Structure

1. Open with a contradiction, consequence or numerical comparison.
2. Give the minimum dated context.
3. Ask one central question.
4. Acknowledge the obvious explanation, then delimit it.
5. Explain actors and structural constraints.
6. Walk through the causal chain with one calculation or comparison.
7. State downstream effects and a conditional conclusion.

## Voice

- Prefer concrete nouns, active verbs and short spoken clauses.
- Use rhetorical questions to organize reasoning, not to replace evidence.
- Use numbers early only when the unit, period and comparison base are known.
- Translate technical mechanisms into one everyday analogy at most.
- Vary transitions; do not repeatedly copy corpus markers such as “这就是” or “总而言之”.
- Preserve uncertainty when sources conflict.

## Default lengths

- Outline: 300–500 Chinese characters.
- 60–90 second script: 450–750 characters.
- 3–5 minute script: 1,000–1,800 characters.
- Deep article: based on evidence, not a fixed target.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- Do not invent a quotation, meeting, investment amount or policy clause.
- Do not cite an aggregator as if it were the original publisher.
- Do not equate market attention with fundamental importance.
- Do not turn a stakeholder's benefit into proof that it caused the event.
- Do not hide failed or stale data collection.
